# External intelligence source register

**Reviewed:** October 16, 2026. This records evidence and limits for the local prototype; it does not grant rights for unrelated third-party data or guarantee production service levels.

| Field                   | USGS earthquake GeoJSON summary feed                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Provider and measure    | U.S. Geological Survey, reported earthquake point, magnitude and depth; observed event, not a damage or shipment-delay forecast                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Endpoint                | [All earthquakes, past day](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/feed/v1.0/summary/all_day.geojson); [format and cadence](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/feed/v1.0/geojson.php)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Schema                  | GeoJSON v1.0 FeatureCollection; `metadata.generated`, feature `id`, `properties.time`, `properties.updated`, `properties.mag`, `properties.url`, point `[longitude, latitude, depth]`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Time and coverage       | Past-day rolling feed, documented as updated every minute. Event origin, event update, feed generation and browser retrieval are distinct. The current feed is not a historical archive or a guarantee that every event is final.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Access                  | Public read-only HTTPS GET, no key. On October 16 a HEAD request returned HTTP 200 and `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`. The app uses a single fixed URL, a 10-second timeout, a response-size and row cap, and no customer data in the request.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Attribution and caution | Show “U.S. Geological Survey (USGS)” and link each event to its USGS record. USGS [data licensing guidance](https://www.usgs.gov/data-management/data-licensing) says federal works are generally public domain but third-party contributions can have restrictions; [USGS provisional-data guidance](https://www.usgs.gov/office-of-science-quality-and-integrity/fundamental-science-practices-fsp-guidance-disclaimer) says timely hazard data may be revised. The app describes reports as preliminary and potential geographic exposure as a screening result. |
| Rights status           | Fetch/display for this public USGS-authored feed: **approved for local prototype** based on official feed and licensing pages. Commercial deployment, redistribution/retention policy and training on records: **pending source-specific review**. No bulk historical storage or model training.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Failure behavior        | Fixed URL, 10-second deadline, size and row caps, stale/invalid/unavailable states; no silent fictional-earthquake fallback. Incomplete features are skipped and reported. A valid empty feed remains empty.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |

Other external sources remain gated by the [unified integration plan](../COST_CURRENT_UNIFIED_INTELLIGENCE_INTEGRATION_CODEX.md). IMF PortWatch needs its own format and rights review before integration.

## NOAA/NHC current tropical cyclone advisories and forecast cones — P2

| Field                       | Status                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Provider and measure        | NOAA/National Hurricane Center `CurrentStorms.json`: active storm IDs, basin, current reported center/intensity, latest public/forecast advisory number and issuance time, and official GIS product links. An advisory is a forecast product, distinct from an observed disruption.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Endpoint and reference      | [CurrentStorms.json](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/CurrentStorms.json), [NHC JSON product reference](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/productexamples/), [NHC GIS formats](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/gis/), [cone interpretation](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/cone_usage.php). Fixed same-origin proxy/function URL; no user-provided upstream URL.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Coverage                    | Atlantic and Eastern Pacific storms in the NHC summary; Central Pacific items are shown only if present with a recognized basin ID. No claim of global tropical cyclone coverage. Valid zero active storms is an empty result, not a safe-all-clear.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Review snapshot             | October 16, 2026 GET returned active EP storms Trudy and Simon, each with latest advisory issuance and official track/cone KMZ URLs. This snapshot is an implementation check, not a fixture or ongoing coverage claim.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Data rights and attribution | NHC/NWS official public information. [NWS use/disclaimer](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/mobile/disclaimer.html) notes service availability and timing are not guaranteed and NWS material is generally not subject to copyright protection. Show “NOAA/National Hurricane Center” and link the official advisory. Prototype fetch/display approved from official pages; commercial redistribution, persistent archive and training use pending source-specific review.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Geometry and limits         | The same-origin endpoint fetches at most five NHC-linked cone KMZ files, rejects non-NHC or advisory-mismatched URLs, bounds compressed and extracted sizes, and checks KML storm ID, advisory number, closed polygon rings and a 12–168-hour cone horizon. Point-in-polygon handles dateline wrap and holes. A known port/site inside the cone is **possible forecast-center-area overlap**, not wind extent, damage, closure probability or confirmed route disruption. Forecast track time-step points and other wind/surge hazard polygons are not integrated. NHC says GIS products are convenience data whose support/timing may vary. |
| Failure behavior            | Fixed URL, 10-second deadlines, size and row caps, stale/invalid/unavailable states; no fictional storm fallback. Missing or invalid KMZ leaves the advisory readable with unknown exposure. Stale advisories suppress cone matching. A static host without the configured same-origin endpoint reports unavailable.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
